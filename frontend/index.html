How this calculation works

What it does: Calculates an illustrative retirement timeline from your inputs: current age, monthly investment amount, desired monthly retirement income, and the annual return scenario you select.

How: Applies a fixed 4% annual withdrawal rate to determine the target portfolio size, then projects time-to-target assuming a constant, reinvested nominal annual return at your selected rate.

Exclusions: Unless otherwise stated, this calculation does not reflect advisory fees, fund expenses, taxes, inflation, changes in contributions or spending, withdrawals, or other personal circumstances. It assumes a constant annual return and does not reflect market volatility, losses, drawdowns, or sequence-of-returns risk.

Variability: Results will vary based on the information entered, the assumptions selected, and changes in market conditions and personal circumstances.

No securities: This tool does not consider, analyze, or recommend any specific securities or investment products.

Not advice: This tool provides general, hypothetical planning information only. It does not provide individualized investment advice or recommend any security, investment product, asset allocation, or investment strategy.

These results are hypothetical illustrations for planning purposes only. They do not reflect actual client performance and are not a prediction or guarantee of future investment results, retirement readiness, or financial independence. The assumed annual return is a hypothetical planning assumption. Actual returns can be lower, higher, or negative and will vary over time. This is not a guarantee that you will be able to retire or reach any goal by the date shown.
