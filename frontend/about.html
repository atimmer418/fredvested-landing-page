Can you trust FRED?

  • Your money, your name

    Your account will be held in your name at Alpaca Securities LLC, a FINRA/SIPC-member brokerage. If FRED were to shut down tomorrow, your money would still be yours. For more information, see the ‘Fair questions’ below.

  • Bank-grade connections

    Your bank connection runs through Plaid, the same rails used by major banking apps. Your bank credentials never touch FRED’s servers.

  • Passkeys, not passwords

    Sign in with Face ID or your fingerprint. There’s no password to steal, phish, or reuse.

  • Withdraw anytime

    Liquid and portable. No lock-ins, no exit fees from FRED, no fine-print hostage-taking.

Fair questions

What happens after I join the waitlist?

You’ll get a confirmation email. When your invite is ready, you can claim one of the 300 Founder Access spots, or pass. Nothing is charged unless you claim.

When do I pay the $99?

Only if you’re invited and choose to claim Founder Access. Joining the waitlist costs nothing, today or ever.

Is my money safe if FRED shuts down?

The digital advisory services provided by FRED may be discontinued in accordance with the advisory agreement. If the service ends, FRED will provide notice through the contact information associated with your account and will explain the effective date of termination, the status of pending transactions, the date automated portfolio-management features will stop, and available account-transition options. Your assets are held at Alpaca Securities LLC, subject to the custodian’s terms and procedures. You may generally retain, transfer, or self-direct your custodial account, subject to applicable restrictions and costs. The discontinuation of FRED’s service does not require you to liquidate investments or transfer assets to a successor adviser. Prior to the discontinuation of FRED’s services, your investment will be subject to risks, including the risk that your investment will lose value because of overall changes in the financial markets.

Why not just buy index funds myself?

You absolutely can. FRED uses plain index funds on purpose. What FRED adds is the automation: every paycheck invested on time, every month, for decades, without relying on willpower. Consistency is what the math rewards.

Why is the beta iOS only?

We’re a small team keeping the beta high-touch for every founding member. Starting iOS-only keeps quality high; Android is on the roadmap.

What does FRED cost after the founder offer?

Flat monthly plans starting at $8 and ranging to $40. Never a percentage of your assets. However your portfolio performs, FRED’s fee stays flat.
