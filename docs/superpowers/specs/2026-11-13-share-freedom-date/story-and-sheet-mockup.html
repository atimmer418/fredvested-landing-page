The link carries your age and date only. No email, no income, no amounts.

Our own sheet because Instagram’s and TikTok’s in-app browsers often lack the system share menu: where it is missing, “Share link” is hidden and “Copy link” leads. “Save image” opens the story image for a long-press save where downloads are blocked.

This direction is for the visitor who wants something to post, not just a link to send: the ticket reads as an object with their date on it, works as a Story without a caption, and gives FRED a recognisable format people can collect and compare. Its main weakness is that the ticket asks more of a small preview than a single big age does: at message-bubble size only the year and the age survive, and the date is the less instantly understood of the two numbers, so a stranger may need the title line to know what “July 2048” means. It is also the most new surface to build and to put past counsel (a sheet, two image sizes, and “13 years before the usual 65” as new wording).
