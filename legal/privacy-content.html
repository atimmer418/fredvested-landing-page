3. How We Collect Your Information

Information you provide to us. We collect information when you interact with our services and use the App. Certain areas of our website and online resources allow you to submit your information to us. We collect your information on the bases of: (i) contract (such as information provided in connection with seeking our services); (ii) consent (such as when you sign up for our services or agree to be included on our mailing list); and (iii) legitimate interest (promoting our services and/or customizing our website).

Automatically through our services. As you navigate our App and use our services, we may use technologies to collect information that may include Personal Information. Information collected automatically may include usage details, IP addresses, operating system, and browser type, and information collected through cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies including details of your interactions with our website. To automatically collect information, we use the techniques described below:

Cookies. Our website may use a tracking technology called a “cookie.” A cookie is a small data file that a website can place on your computer’s hard drive where your internet browser files are kept. This file is then stored on your computer. Cookies enable us to recognize when a computer being used to browse our website has previously visited the website or online resource. Cookies can also be used to enable a website to “remember” the information a visitor has previously inputted. Cookies may be placed on your computer by our security and hosting provider as a necessary security measure. Our website also uses Cloudflare Turnstile to distinguish human visitors from automated traffic. Cloudflare's collection and processing of data through Turnstile is described in the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum, available at https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/.

Most internet browsers allow you to change your settings to notify you when you receive a cookie, which lets you choose whether or not to accept it, or to automatically refuse all cookies. Please be aware that some functionality on our website and online resources relies on the use of cookies and may not be available should you choose to refuse all cookies. You may also delete and manage cookies through your internet browser.

Web Beacons. On certain web pages or in e-mails we send to you, we and/or our analytics provider may utilize a technology called a “web beacon” (also known as an “action tag”, “pixel tags,” “single-pixel GIFs” or “clear GIF technology”). Web beacons also help analyze the effectiveness of the website by measuring the number of visitors to a website or how many visitors clicked on key elements of a website.

If you click a link in a marketing e-mail or invitation, we may track this action through a web beacon. You may avoid having this activity tracked by visiting our website without using the link and then navigating to the content you are interested in.

Browser Storage. We store UTM attribution in your browser’s local Storage and session Storage.

Do Not Track Signals. Our website does not support “Do Not Track” browser settings at this time.

4. How We Store And How Long We Retain Your Information

Your data is stored on a MySQL database hosted on Railway, a cloud infrastructure service, connected to our Java Spring Boot backend. We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and access controls to protect your data.

Sensitive data handling:

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text
  • SSNs and sensitive KYC data are encrypted at rest
  • Access to production data is limited to authorized personnel only

Federal law requires FRED to maintain records related to its provision of investment advice. As a result, while you may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 8), we will need to retain certain data even after the receipt of a deletion request to satisfy our regulatory obligations.

We will retain our email marketing list for as long as the business is in operation. This includes keeping the list of addresses that have opted out from our marketing so that we do not contact them again.

Retention periods may vary depending upon the nature of the information and applicable legal requirements.

Our analytics provider is provided with information on page views, referrer sites, browser types, operating systems, device types, and the country/region/city of connections. We also forward the visitor’s IP address to the analytics provider, which uses it to derive location and to generate a daily-rotating visitor hash for counting unique visitors. Our analytics provider does not store IP addresses and the salt for the visitor hash is deleted every 24 hours.

We may also retain information if it is required to resolve disputes, enforce our contractual rights, or because it is the subject of a government inquiry. In these instances, we will limit our retention to what is reasonably required to address the dispute and any potential follow up.
